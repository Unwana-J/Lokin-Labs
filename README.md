## 🎨 Design Philosophy & Architectural Rules

- **Zero "AI Bubble" Pills**: No generic bubbly pill badges; strictly architectural lines, delicate 1px dividers, and monospaced technical metadata.
- **60-30-10 Color Science**:
  - `60%` Warm organic canvas (`#FAF7F0`)
  - `30%` Deep botanical olive (`#141B0E` / `#1E2714`)
  - `10%` High-contrast purposeful accents (Terracotta Crimson `#9B283A`, Chartreuse `#C4E456`, Ochre `#D99B26`)
- **Foundry Leadership Attribution**:
  - The studio speaks in the collective plural (*"We get an idea..."*).
  - The **400+ financial institutions, BaaS, and core banking track record** is attributed strictly to the leadership DNA of the team lead, **Unwana John-King**.
- **Official Inquiries**: `letslokin@gmail.com`
